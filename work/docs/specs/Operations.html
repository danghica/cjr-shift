executes_result

Tracker.Operations.executes_result

AI-generated explanation

The lemma states that if expression e evaluates to value v under the supplied executes relation, and v equals w, then e also evaluates to w. It simply allows an evaluation result to be replaced by an equal value; it does not assert that every expression terminates or that evaluation results are unique.
